Privacy Policy

MovieBox · Last updated 1 November 2026

MovieBox is a movie discovery and review app. This policy explains what the app stores on your device, what it shares with third-party services, and the choices available to you.

In short: MovieBox has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your watchlist and settings stay on your device. The app uses Google services for advertising, usage statistics (including the movies you view and the terms you search for) and crash reporting, and requests movie information from TMDB. None of this is linked to your name or email address.

The following is saved only on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

2. Information collected by third-party services

MovieBox does not operate its own servers. The services below run inside the app and handle data under their own privacy policies.

Google AdMob (advertising)

The app is free and supported by advertising. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where you have allowed them.

  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for advertising consent when you first open the app.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices).
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Firebase Analytics (Google)

Analytics helps us understand how the app is used so we can improve it. Along with standard information such as device model, operating system version, country and an app instance identifier, the app records these events:

  • Movies you open (the movie's TMDB ID and title)
  • Terms you type into search
  • Trailers you open and movies you add to your watchlist (by TMDB ID)
  • Whether ads were requested and shown

Because the app has no accounts, this information is not linked to your name, email address or phone number.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google)

If the app crashes, Crashlytics sends a report containing the error, the device model and the operating system version. It is used only to find and fix faults.

Firebase Remote Config (Google)

The app downloads its settings (for example, how often ads are shown) from Remote Config. The request identifies the app installation but contains no personal information.

Firebase privacy and security

TMDB (The Movie Database)

Movie information, artwork, cast details and reviews are requested from the TMDB API. When you search, your search term is sent to TMDB to find matching movies. These requests include your IP address, as any internet request does. MovieBox does not send TMDB your watchlist or any other information about you.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. TMDB privacy policy

YouTube (trailers)

Trailers are hosted on YouTube. Opening a trailer hands it to the YouTube app, or to your browser if YouTube is not installed, where Google's privacy policy applies.

Google Play (in-app review)

The app may ask Google Play to show its standard rating prompt. Google decides whether the prompt appears, and MovieBox is not told what you rate or write.

4. Your choices

  • Notifications — turn them on or off at any time in your device's app settings.
  • Ad personalisation — change it in your device's ad settings, or reset your advertising ID.
  • Advertising consent — where a consent form was shown, you can change your choice by clearing the app's storage, which shows the form again on the next launch.
  • Stored data — clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes your watchlist, searches and settings permanently.

5. Children

MovieBox is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Movie information comes from TMDB and may include titles intended for adult audiences.

6. Data retention and security

Data on your device remains until you delete it. Data held by Google and TMDB is retained under their own policies. Because MovieBox has no account system and no server, there is no profile of you for us to store, expose or delete.

7. Streaming

MovieBox is a discovery and review app. It does not host, stream, download or provide access to films. The Watch button opens a film's official trailer on YouTube.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app develops. The date at the top of the page shows when it last changed.
